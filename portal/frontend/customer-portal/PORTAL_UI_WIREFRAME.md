# DutyGuard-AI Customer Portal UI/UX Wireframe

## Color Palette (Color Psychology)
- **Primary:** #2563eb (Blue: trust, security, professionalism)
- **Accent:** #f59e42 (Orange: action, optimism) or #22c55e (Green: growth, success)
- **CTA Buttons:** #f59e42 or #22c55e (bold, high-contrast)
- **Background:** #f9fafb (very light gray)
- **Highlight:** #fde047 (Yellow: urgency, special offers)
- **Text:** #111827 (dark gray/black)

## Portal Layout

### Header
- Logo (left)
- Navigation: Home | Upload | Quotes | Invoices | Results | Support
- Login/Signup (right)

### Hero Section
- Welcome message: “Unlock Your Tariff Data Instantly”
- “First 2 Quotes Free!” badge (yellow highlight)
- Upload button (large, orange/green CTA)

### Main Dashboard
- **Progress Bar:** Shows steps (Upload → Quote → Payment → Results)
-- **Current Status:** “You have 1 free quote left!”
- **Recent Activity:** List of uploads, quotes, invoices, results
- **CTA:** “Get Started” or “Upload Now”

### Social Proof
- Customer testimonials, logos, case studies

### Footer
- Privacy Policy | Terms | Contact | Social links

### Other Features
- Live chat widget (bottom right)
- Notification bell for updates
- Analytics dashboard (admin only)

---

*This wireframe is designed to maximize trust, engagement, and conversion using proven color psychology and customer-centric UX.*
