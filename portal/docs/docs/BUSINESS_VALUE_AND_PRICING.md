# Why Customers Pay for DutyGuard-AI

- **Expertise & Automation:** You provide expert analysis, automation, and structured data that customers can’t easily get themselves.
- **Cost Coverage:** Your fees cover your time, hosting, development, and ongoing support.
- **Business Validation:** Charging for your service validates your business, enabling you to grow, improve, and support more customers.
- **Professional Expectation:** Customers expect to pay for specialized, accurate, and timely information.

# Pricing Models (Beyond Tariff Findings)

Consider charging for:

- **Data Upload & Processing:** Charge per document, per page, or per data batch processed.
- **Subscription Access:** Monthly/annual fee for ongoing access to the platform, updates, and support.
- **API Access:** Charge for API usage (per call, per data volume, or tiered plans).
- **Custom Reports:** Extra fee for custom analysis, reports, or data exports.
- **Priority Support:** Premium for faster turnaround or dedicated support.
- **Consulting:** Hourly/project-based fees for expert advice or integration help.
- **White-labeling:** Higher fee for branded/customized solutions.

Mix and match these models to fit your customer base and business goals.
